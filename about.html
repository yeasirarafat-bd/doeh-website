Journey of DOEH

From a strong foundation in public health to field-focused education, research, and professional impact.

2012
Department established
The Department of Occupational and Environmental Health and Safety was founded under the Faculty of Public Health at Bangladesh University of Health Sciences, established by the Diabetic Association of Bangladesh.
2013
Academic program began
The BPH in Occupational and Environmental Health and Safety program commenced, bringing structured, field-focused public health education to students across Bangladesh.
Today, DOEH remains committed to advancing education, research, and field-based learning, preparing public health professionals to address real-world occupational and environmental health challenges.
What drives us
Mission & Vision
Our mission
What we are here to do

To develop skilled public health professionals capable of effectively addressing occupational and environmental health issues at both national and global levels, to prevent workplace hazards, protect health, and promote safe, sustainable environments.

Our vision
Where we are heading

To be a leading center of excellence in occupational and environmental health, pioneering innovative approaches and producing skilled professionals dedicated to healthy environments and safer workplaces, recognized at national and global levels.

The three dimensions of
learning at DOEH

What makes us different
Field
Field-Focused Learning

Academic knowledge meets real workplaces, industries, and environments. Students visit factories, construction sites, and environmental zones, not just classrooms. Learning happens where the risk actually is.

Research
Evidence, Research & Innovation

From thesis work to faculty publications, DOEH builds a culture where questions drive practice. Students engage with real research methodology, data analysis, and the kind of evidence that shapes real health and safety policy.

Impact
Healthier Workplaces & Sustainable Environments

The end goal is not a degree. It is a profession that protects people. Graduates enter sectors ranging from international organizations to government and industry, where their work directly reduces harm.

IOSH
Accredited Programs
International recognition

Internationally Recognized. Professionally Relevant.

Our BPH and MPH in Occupational Health and Safety programs carry IOSH accreditation, an internationally recognized standard in occupational safety and health. IOSH, the Institution of Occupational Safety and Health, is the world's largest professional body for health and safety practitioners. Accreditation means our graduates are recognized by employers and institutions globally, not just at home.

Where the learning actually happens

Facilities built for hands-on work, practical skills, and real-world learning.

Environmental Lab
Analyze, monitor, and understand environmental health risks through hands-on testing and measurement.
Occupational Lab
Explore workplace safety, occupational hazards, ergonomics, and risk control in a dedicated learning environment.
GIS Lab
Spatial tools for mapping, visualizing, and analyzing health and environmental data across geographic areas.
Fire Safety & Emergency Response Lab
Practical training in fire prevention, emergency preparedness, response procedures, and workplace safety.
Computer Lab
Data analysis, modelling, statistical software, and digital learning support for research and coursework.
