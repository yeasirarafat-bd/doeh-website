    BPHIOSH Accredited

    Bachelor of Public Health, Occupational & Environmental Health and Safety

    If you want to start from the beginning and build a full career in this field, this is your program. Hands-on lab and field work from day one, so you graduate ready to walk onto a construction site, into a factory, or straight into a master's program, at home or abroad.

    4 yrsDuration
    8Semesters
    160Credits
    48Courses

    Educational objectives

    This program prepares graduates to explain and address occupational and environmental health issues through a combination of scientific knowledge, practical fieldwork, and policy understanding. Students develop skills in hazard identification, risk assessment, workplace safety management, and environmental impact analysis, grounded in both national and international standards.

    Admission eligibility

    • SSC (Science) and HSC (Science) or equivalent, with a minimum CGPA of 2.5 in either and a combined CGPA of 5.0
    • O/A-Level: minimum 5 O-Level and 2 A-Level subjects; Grade B (GPA 4.00) in at least 4 subjects, Grade C (GPA 3.50) in 3 subjects
    • Four-year Diploma in MATS, IHT, or other relevant disciplines also considered
    • Foreign students with equivalent qualifications are eligible; equivalence decided by BUHS's Equivalence Committee

    Where BPH graduates work

    • Mega construction projects
    • Oil, gas & power supply sector
    • Garments, jute & leather industry
    • Steel & engineering industry
    • Food & chemical industry
    • Environment protection & disaster management
    • Government & NGOs
    • Universities, research centers & consulting firms
    • International organizations & development agencies

    MPH-OHSIOSH Accredited

    Master of Public Health, Occupational Health & Safety

    Already have a foundation in the field and want to specialize further. A focused, 1.5-year program covering everything from workplace hazard and risk management to fire safety, ergonomics, and occupational toxicology, built for people ready to move straight into a specialist or leadership role.

    1.5 yrsDuration
    3Semesters
    70Credits
    20Courses

    Educational objectives

    This program develops specialists in occupational health and safety who can assess workplace hazards, implement safety management systems, and apply evidence-based approaches to preventing occupational illness and injury across diverse industries.

    Admission eligibility

    • Honours in Occupational Health and Safety or its equivalent from any recognized university
    • Four years university graduation in any science programme, or graduation in engineering/agriculture with at least 2nd class (or equivalent GPA) from a recognized university
    • MBBS or its equivalent degree from a recognized university
    • Foreign students with equivalent qualifications are eligible; equivalence decided by BUHS's Equivalence Committee
    • Minimum CGPA of 2.00 in the Honours program is required for admission

    Where MPH-OHS graduates work

    • Mega construction projects
    • Oil, gas & power supply sector
    • Garments, jute & leather industry
    • Steel & engineering industry
    • Food & chemical industry
    • Environment protection & disaster management
    • Government & NGOs
    • Universities, research centers & consulting firms
    • International organizations & development agencies

    MPH-EHS

    Master of Public Health, Environmental Health & Safety

    For people drawn to the environmental side of this field specifically: pollution, climate impact, ecological risk. A 2-year program combining fieldwork, lab work, and research, with faculty actively publishing in peer-reviewed journals and taking part in international collaborations.

    2 yrsDuration
    4Semesters
    80Credits
    21Courses

    Educational objectives

    This program equips graduates to understand environmental health risks, assess ecological impacts, and develop strategies for environmental protection and sustainability, with a focus on connecting scientific knowledge to policy and real-world environmental challenges.

    Admission eligibility

    • Honours in Environmental Health and Safety or Environmental Science, or its equivalent, from any recognized university
    • Four years university graduation in any science programme, or graduation in engineering/agriculture with at least 2nd class (or equivalent GPA) from a recognized university
    • MBBS or its equivalent degree from a recognized university
    • Foreign students with equivalent qualifications are eligible; equivalence decided by BUHS's Equivalence Committee
    • Minimum CGPA of 2.00 in the Honours program is required for admission

    Where MPH-EHS graduates work

    • Environmental health specialist, research program manager, environmental health & safety manager, industrial hygienist, or research assistant
    • Academic roles in colleges, institutes, and universities
    • Skills relevant to international OSH/EHS roles and further study
    • Roles across various government departments
    • Higher education (PhD) and training opportunities, at home and abroad
