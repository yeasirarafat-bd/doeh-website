    Who we are

    The Department of Occupational and Environmental Health, established under the Faculty of Public Health at Bangladesh University of Health Sciences, prepares graduates to identify, evaluate, and control workplace and environmental health hazards through rigorous academic training and field research.

    Learn more about DOEH
    Our mission

    "To develop skilled public health professionals capable of effectively and efficiently addressing occupational and environmental health issues at both national and global levels."

    Our vision

    To be recognized as a global leader in occupational and environmental health, pioneering innovative approaches and producing skilled professionals dedicated to sustainable healthy environments and safer workplaces.

    What we offer

    Three IOSH-recognized programs designed to build professionals in occupational and environmental health, combining theory, fieldwork, and research.
