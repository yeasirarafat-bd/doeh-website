About DOEH Alumni Association

Connecting graduates of the Department of Occupational and Environmental Health, BUHS — building a stronger professional community.

🎓

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between students and working professionals, creating a network of mentorship and opportunity in occupational and environmental health.

🌐

The Directory

A live, searchable database of DOEH alumni — their current roles, organizations, expertise and contact details — updated as new members register.

🤝

For Students

Browse alumni profiles to understand career paths, reach out for mentorship and discover opportunities in your field of interest.

📬

Stay Connected

Keep your profile up to date so classmates and upcoming students can find and learn from your career journey. Together we grow the DOEH legacy.
